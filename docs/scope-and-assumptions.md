# Scope and Assumptions

## Initial scope

- Personal account with ₹1,00,000 capital
- Indian NSE cash equities and liquid ETFs
- Positional/swing trading
- No leverage, futures, or options in the first release
- Research and paper/shadow trading only
- Zerodha integration is a later phase

## Explicitly out of scope for the foundation

- Live order placement
- Zerodha credentials or tokens
- Guaranteed returns or a mandatory monthly profit quota
- Managing money for others
- Selling signals or providing investment advice
- Unrestricted autonomous LLM trading

## Acceptance approach

A strategy must pass data-quality checks, realistic backtesting, out-of-sample/walk-forward evaluation, deterministic risk tests, and paper/shadow operation before small-capital live consideration.
